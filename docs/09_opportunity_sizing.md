# 9. Opportunity Sizing

Slide 13.

## Potential business value
- **Time saved:**
  - research drops from about **3 hours a week** by hand to about **0.5 hours** reading the dashboard;
  - that saves **2.5 hours a week = 130 hours a year**.
- **Better topics (not counted yet):**
  - picks that people keep asking about (7 in 10 vs under 5 in 10 for random) should lead to more views;
  - but comments are not views, so this is left for the A/B test (section 11) to prove.
- **Opportunity vs existing tools:**
  - vidIQ-type tools look at searches, and comment tools look at one video;
  - this tool finds what viewers across channels ask that no video answers yet, and it is back-tested.

## Assumptions
| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Research by hand today | 3 h a week (my own estimate) |
| Research with the dashboard | 0.5 h a week (read the Top 3 and the evidence) |
| Build time | about 50 h |
| Upkeep | 2 h a month: rename topics after monthly re-clustering, add labels, log results |
| Running cost | free API quota (one weekly run uses about 7,000 of 10,000 daily units); runs on a laptop in about 10 minutes; an optional small cloud VM is about S$10 a month |

## Value vs cost (in my own time)
| | Hours |
|---|---|
| Time saved per year | 130 |
| Upkeep per year (2 h × 12) | 24 |
| **Net saved per year** | **106** (about 9 h a month) |
| One-off build | 50 |
| **Pays back in** | **about 6 months** (50 ÷ 9) |

Even if it saves only 1 hour a week (52 h a year), it still covers the 24 hours of upkeep.
